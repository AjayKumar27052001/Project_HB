# Juniper Hotels Guest Loyalty Program & Upselling Matrix

<!-- [TIER_BENEFITS] -->
## Membership Tiers & Privileges

### Silver Member
- **Qualification:** Free instant registration (0 qualifying nights).
- **Earning Rate:** 5 points per ₹100 of eligible spending on room charges and dining.
- **Benefits:** Member-only promotional rates, priority notification for flash sales.

### Gold Member
- **Qualification:** 5 qualifying nights annually.
- **Earning Rate:** 7 points per ₹100 of eligible spending.
- **Benefits:**
  - 5% exclusive direct-booking discount on all flexible room rates.
  - Priority consideration for late checkout (up to 2:00 PM upon request).
  - 1 Complimentary Room Upgrade Award after completing 5 qualifying nights.

### Platinum Member
- **Qualification:** 15 qualifying nights annually.
- **Earning Rate:** 10 points per ₹100 of eligible spending.
- **Benefits:**
  - 10% exclusive direct-booking discount on all flexible room rates.
  - Complimentary daily hot breakfast for up to 2 registered guests per room.
  - Guaranteed room upgrades to next best category (including Deluxe Suites, subject to availability).
  - Guaranteed late checkout up to 2:00 PM.
  - 2 Complimentary Room Upgrade Awards annually.
<!-- [END_TIER_BENEFITS] -->

## Points Redemption & Program Rules

### Points Valuation & Redemption
- **Cash Value:** ₹0.20 per point (e.g., 500 points = ₹100 in booking or dining credit; 12,400 points = ₹2,480 credit).
- Points can be redeemed toward future room reservations, room upgrades, or dining folio credits.

### Stacking & Exclusion Rules
- **Non-Stacking:** Loyalty direct booking discounts (5% Gold, 10% Platinum) cannot be stacked with third-party promo codes or seasonal flash promotions.
- **Ineligible Stays:** Cancelled reservations, refunded charges, and bookings made via third-party OTAs (Expedia, Booking.com) do not earn loyalty points or count toward tier progression.
- **Point Expiration:** Points remain active for 24 months from the date of the last qualifying activity.

## Upselling & Cross-Selling Matrix

### Post-Booking Offers (Confirmation Stage)
1. **Room Upgrades:**
   - Standard Room → Deluxe Room or Junior Suite.
   - Cost: +₹1,500 per night.
   - *Perk:* Gold and Platinum members may apply an available Upgrade Award to receive this upgrade free of charge.
2. **Breakfast Package:**
   - Daily gourmet breakfast buffet.
   - Cost: +₹500 per guest per day.
   - *Perk:* Complimentary for Platinum members.
3. **Private Airport Transportation:**
   - Dedicated private chauffeur pickup or drop-off between airport and hotel.
   - Cost: +₹1,200 per one-way trip.
4. **Special Occasion Celebration Package:**
   - Includes custom room decoration, celebratory pastry/cake, and floral arrangement (Anniversary, Birthday, Honeymoon).
   - Cost: +₹2,000 per stay package.
5. **Late Checkout Package:**
   - Guaranteed late departure up to 2:00 PM.
   - Cost: +₹800 per stay.
   - *Perk:* Complimentary priority benefit for Gold and Platinum members.
